Masking test

1 · Hearing threshold

Use headphones in a quiet place. Set your device volume to a normal level and do not change it during the test.

Press Play, then raise the level slowly from the left until you can just hear the beeps.

2 · Masking level

Listen to your own tinnitus (no sound from tinytus). Raise the noise slowly until you no longer hear your tinnitus. Stop at the lowest level that does it.

Result

A self-test with your own device: useful to follow your tinnitus over time, not a replacement for a hearing test by a professional.
